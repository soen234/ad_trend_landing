# Privacy Policy for TAT (Today Ad Trend)

**Effective date: October 25, 2026**

This policy explains what information TAT ("Today Ad Trend", "we", "our" or "us") handles when you use the Today Ad Trend mobile app ("the app") and the website at todayadtrend.com ("the website"), and the choices you have.

## The short version

- There are no user accounts. You never sign up, log in or give us your name or email to use the app.
- We do not run servers that store personal data. Everything you set up in the app stays on your device.
- Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push token is sent anywhere.
- The app shows Google AdMob ads using non-personalized requests only. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- The website shows Google AdSense ads and uses Vercel Analytics.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps these settings and activity on your device only:

- the role you choose (Performance, UA / App, or Brand & creative)
- your briefing reminder time and whether reminders are on
- which items you have read and which you have saved
- your watchlist keywords
- your reading streak

We cannot see this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Briefing content and network requests

The app downloads the daily briefing as public files (JSON) from GitHub Pages (soen234.github.io), a static hosting service run by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, device or browser type and the time of the request to operate and secure its service. These requests do not include anything that identifies you in the app. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The briefing consists of short AI-generated summaries of public RSS feeds from publishers, platforms and newsletters, with links to the original articles. No information about you is used to generate it. When you open a link, you leave the app or website and the publisher's own privacy policy applies.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your device for notification permission and schedules a local notification at the time you choose. The notification is created on your device. We do not collect or store a push token or any notification data.

## Advertising in the app (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob outside the daily brief. Ad requests are sent as non-personalized requests, so Google does not use them to build a profile of your interests. Google may still collect and use limited information, such as your IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, to deliver ads, measure performance, limit how often you see an ad and prevent fraud. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, so on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may read the device's advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention; you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## The website

### Google AdSense

The website shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can manage personalized advertising in [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows personalized ads only where consent has been given; otherwise it shows non-personalized or limited ads.

### Vercel Analytics and hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel and uses Vercel Analytics to count page views. Vercel Analytics does not use cookies and reports aggregated data, such as which pages are visited and the type of device and country of visitors. Vercel may process server logs, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. See the [Vercel Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

### Language preference

If you switch the website language, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your browser.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information.
- We do not use your information for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

## Data retention

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google, GitHub and Vercel is retained according to their own policies.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete app data:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your device settings.
- **Notifications:** turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.
- **Website ads:** manage ad personalization in Google's Ad Settings or block cookies in your browser.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests can be handled by the steps above. You can still contact us at any time, and we will help where we can.

## Children's privacy

The app and website are intended for marketing professionals and are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or website changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
